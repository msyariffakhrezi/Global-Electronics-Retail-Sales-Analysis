## Project Background
IC Electronics is a US-based retail company that sells a wide range of electronic goods online and in-store accross various countries.

IC Electronics now has more than 15,000 customers and has sold nearly 194,000 of its products over the last 5 years, generating sales revenue of over $9 million. The available data covers a various dimensions and metrics, including sales, products, customers, and stores. This project thoroughly analyses this data in order to uncover critical insights that will improve company's commercial performance.

Insights and recommendations are provided on the following key areas:
- **Sales Trend Analysis**: Identification of historical sales patterns, focusing on sales revenue, number of orders, and average order value (AOV).
- **Product Performance**: An analysis of company's various product lines, understanding the impact on sales.
- **Region Store Performance**: An assessment of each store's contribution to sales revenue.
- **Customer Segment Comparisons**: An evaluation of which customer segment have the greatest impact on sales, to help determine the right strategy.
