## Project Background
IC Electronics is a US-based retail company that sells a wide range of electronic goods in various countries.

IC Electronics now has more than 15,000 customers and has sold nearly 194,000 of its products over the last 5 years, generating sales revenue of over $9 million. The available data covers a various dimensions and metrics, including sales, products, customers, and stores. This project thoroughly analyses this data in order to uncover critical insights that will improve company's commercial performance.

- Sales Trend Analysis: Identification of historical sales patterns, focusing on sales revenue, number of orders, and average order value (AOV).
